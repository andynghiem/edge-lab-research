# Published Case Studies

EDGE LAB publishes both positive and negative research outcomes when they add useful evidence.

| ID | Study | Research verdict | Deployment verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0030 | [WBETH staking-enhanced carry](0030-wbeth-staking-carry/) | `WBETH_STAKING_CARRY_CANDIDATE` | `DEPLOYMENT_REJECTED_CAPITAL_EFFICIENCY` |

## Interpretation

A research `CANDIDATE` means the preregistered historical mechanism survived its published gates. It does not mean the strategy is guaranteed, safe, suitable for live trading or economically attractive enough to deploy.

The deployment verdict is deliberately separate so the repository can preserve useful scientific results without turning every statistically positive result into a trading recommendation.
